# Don't Break It - Support

Email: koray.akkilic007@icloud.com

## Restore purchases
Open the Shop or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Your Remove Ads unlock and character bundles are restored to the Apple ID you purchased with.

## Remove Ads
Remove Ads permanently disables full-screen interstitial ads. Optional rewarded videos stay available because they are always your choice and grant a bonus.

## Progress
Progress is saved on your device and survives app updates. Deleting the app removes it.

## Stuck on a level
All 60 levels are verified solvable by an automated solver. Try moving one object a small distance - the physics is sensitive to angles.
