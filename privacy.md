# Don't Break It - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Don't Break It is a single-player puzzle game. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game and show ads.

## What we do NOT collect
We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any account. There is no login. We do not sell personal data.

## Stored on your device only
Your level progress, stars, coins, gems, unlocked characters and settings are saved locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Purchases
In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase or restore succeeded.

## Advertising
The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier and general usage signals to serve and measure ads. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

On iOS you control tracking under Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. You can also buy Remove Ads to disable full-screen ads.

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages and contains no chat, user content or social features.
